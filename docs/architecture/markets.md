# Markets: perps and predictions

Aura gives customers perpetual futures on Hyperliquid and prediction markets on Polymarket. Aura is a front end, not a broker: the customer's own Privy wallet owns the account at each venue, and Aura never holds or moves their money. Code is in `apps/web/src/lib/markets/`, API routes are under `/api/perps/*` and `/api/predictions/*`, and two switches gate them: `perps` and `predictions` ([launch controls](../operations/launch-controls.md)). Both are off by default.

Owner decisions (3 October 2026): no Aura fee, no leverage cap beyond each market's own maximum, and no sports or esports markets.

**Where they work.** Aura's server signs or sends these orders, so a venue never sees the customer's location and its own check can't apply. Aura applies each venue's published list instead (owner decision, 3 October 2026; `providerRestrictedPlace` in `lib/legal/places.ts`): Hyperliquid's for Perps (the United States and its territories, and Ontario), and Polymarket's for Predictions (every place it's close-only on its site or API, such as the United States, the United Kingdom, France, Germany, Australia, Singapore, and four Canadian provinces), plus the United Arab Emirates, which Aura adds on its own (owner, 4 October 2026). From those places, setup, adding money, and opening or adding to a position answer 451 `place_restricted`; closing, reduce-only orders, cancelling, redeeming, and withdrawing always work. The place is Cloudflare's geolocation of the request; the Worker passes the region on in `X-Aura-Region`, replacing any value a client sent.

## Who holds what

| | Hyperliquid (perps) | Polymarket (predictions) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Venue account | The customer's Privy wallet address on HyperCore | A Polymarket Deposit Wallet on Polygon, owned by the customer's Privy wallet |
| Signs orders | A trading key the customer's browser makes and keeps (IndexedDB, encrypted with a non-exportable WebCrypto key). The customer's wallet approves it once per device (`approveAgent`). Aura never has it, so Aura can't trade for anyone. Hyperliquid never lets it withdraw or transfer. | The customer's wallet, with their passkey, for each order. Session keys need Polymarket's approval of Aura's builder key. |
| Signs withdrawals | The customer's wallet only | The customer's wallet only |
| Aura keeps | The address of the trading key approved last (no key) | The Deposit Wallet address and its order-book API key, encrypted with `MARKETS_CREDENTIAL_KEY`. The key can read and cancel orders but can't sign one or move funds. |

Everything the customer's wallet signs follows one path (`lib/markets/signing.ts`). The server builds the exact EIP-712 data and keeps it for 5 minutes in `market_signature_requests`. The browser signs Privy's authorization request with the passkey, and the server relays it, so Privy signs only that data, once. A Polymarket signature is also recovered and checked against the owner before it is sent.

D1 never owns money here either. `market_accounts` says which venue account the wallet uses; `market_operations` records what Aura sent and the venue's answer (source, external ID, status, `observedAt`). Balances, positions, orders, and fills are read from the venue on every request, and a failed read is shown as unavailable.

## Perps on Hyperliquid

1. **Add money.** `POST /api/perps/deposit` prepares a route action from Base USDC into the same wallet's perps balance. Relay goes first (`lib/markets/relay.ts`): the wallet approves and deposits the exact amount into Relay's depository on Base (`0x4cd0…bc31`, checked against the quote's calldata), and Relay's solver pays the same amount less its fee, a few cents, into the perps balance in about a second. It settles when Relay reports the fill for this Base transaction and account and Hyperliquid's ledger shows the credit under the hash Relay paid with, for at least the quoted minimum. If Relay can't fill, it refunds on Base and the action fails as `refunded`. If Relay can't quote, Circle's CCTP does it: a fast burn on Base (to HyperEVM, domain 19) whose hook tells Circle's forwarder to credit the perps balance, in seconds, for about 25 cents; it settles when Circle's message for this burn names the customer's perps balance and is forwarded, and Hyperliquid's ledger shows the credit (`verifyCctpDeposit`, see [money actions](money-actions.md)). HyperCore's ledger hash is its own, so a CCTP credit is matched by amount, recipient, and time. Neither waits for the Base block to be final: once Hyperliquid shows the credit, the money has arrived. A mismatch or failure still waits for finality before the action fails. Aura takes no fee. Hyperliquid needs at least 5 USDC to arrive, so the minimum deposit is 6 USDC. (Measured 3 October 2026, Base to perps: Relay $0.02 on $8 and $0.03 on $100, about a second; Circle $0.26 to $0.27, seconds; LI.FI adds 0.25%, and its default route took about 18 minutes. Hyperliquid's own bridge is free but only from Arbitrum.)
2. **Set up.** The browser makes its trading key and sends its address to `POST /api/perps/setup`. If Hyperliquid already lists it, the device is ready; otherwise the route returns `approveAgent` for the passkey and `POST /api/perps/signatures` sends it. Hyperliquid accepts this only after the first deposit. Each device gets its own named slot (`Aura 1` to `Aura 3`); with Hyperliquid's 3 named slots full, Aura's oldest key is replaced, and another app's key is never touched. A key Aura's server held before October 2026 is replaced by the first device key, under its name. The new key then signs standard account mode (`agentSetAbstraction`), where each dex keeps its own USDC balance.
3. **Trade.** `POST /api/perps/trade` takes what the customer picks: market, long or short, a dollar margin, leverage, cross or isolated, market or limit, and an optional take profit and stop loss. It sets the market's leverage and margin mode, moves margin into a stock market's dex if that dex is short (`agentSendAsset`, which only moves money within the account), then places the order with the auto-close orders attached. `POST /api/perps/trade/preview` returns the size, notional, margin, and estimated liquidation price without placing anything. `/positions/tpsl` sets auto-close on an open position; `/orders`, `/orders/cancel`, `/positions/close`, and `/leverage` cover the rest. Each of these routes runs Aura's checks (switches, locks, places, sizing) and answers `202` with the actions it built (`{ status: "sign", requestId, owner, typedData[] }`) instead of sending them. The browser signs each with its trading key, no passkey, and posts the signatures to `POST /api/perps/relay`, which sends exactly the stored actions to Hyperliquid in order, once, within 5 minutes, and only if one key signed them all. A trade's leverage, margin move, and order are signed together. When Hyperliquid doesn't know the key (another device took its slot, or the customer revoked it), the relay answers `409 perps_not_connected` and the browser connects again, then retries once. Market orders are immediate-or-cancel at the mid price ± 1%, as Hyperliquid's own SDKs place them. Orders under $10 are refused unless reduce-only.
4. **Withdraw.** `POST /api/perps/withdraw` asks the customer's wallet to sign `sendToEvmWithData`, which Circle's CCTP delivers as USDC to the same address on Base.

Markets include Hyperliquid's own and the HIP-3 dexes margined in USDC (stock perps such as `xyz:SPCX`), with sports screened out by dex and market name. An order's asset ID for a HIP-3 market is `100000 + dex index × 10000 + index`. Estimated liquidation prices use Hyperliquid's documented formula with a maintenance rate of 1 / (2 × max leverage); margin tiers for very large positions aren't modelled. For cross, the account value is what's there now plus only what has to come in first (the part of the margin beyond what's free to trade, which a one-tap deposit or a move into a stock market's dex adds); the margin itself is money already in the account.

`GET /api/perps/markets` lists markets and prices (public; an isolate reuses the list for 5 seconds, since listing every dex spends Hyperliquid's per-IP budget). `GET /api/perps/account` reads margin per dex, positions, open orders, and fills. `GET /api/perps/candles?coin=&range=` returns chart candles for Live, 1H, 1D, 1W, 1M, 3M, 1Y, or All, and `GET /api/perps/book?coin=` returns the order book and spread, grouped when asked as Hyperliquid groups it (`sig` of 2 to 5 significant figures, and at 5 a `mantissa` step of 2 or 5); both are public, read Hyperliquid on each request, and check the coin against the cached market list so polling costs one read. The trade preview also estimates the fee from the account's own Hyperliquid rate (`userFees`, after its referral discount), doubled for stock markets, whose deployer takes half; it shows as unavailable if Hyperliquid doesn't answer. Aura adds no fee.

## Predictions on Polymarket

1. **Set up.** `POST /api/predictions/setup` takes one step per call: deploy the Deposit Wallet through Polymarket's relayer (Aura's builder key pays the gas), then the wallet's contract approvals (one passkey), then the order-book sign-in (one passkey). The order-book key is sealed and stored.
2. **Add money.** `POST /api/predictions/deposit` prepares a transfer of Base USDC to the wallet's Polymarket bridge address, which credits it as pUSD. It is the customer's own money moving between their accounts, so it doesn't count toward the daily limit; `predictions` gates it.
3. **Trade.** `POST /api/predictions/orders/buy` prices a dollar amount against the book and returns the order for the passkey, with the estimated shares and the payout if it wins. On markets with Polymarket's taker fee (read from the CLOB's market parameters, such as Up or Down), the amount covers shares and fee together: the server prices the whole amount, then spends it less that fee, and returns the fee in the quote. `/orders/sell` sells shares at the best bid less 2%. `POST /api/predictions/signatures` posts the signed order. `/orders/cancel` cancels a resting one.
4. **Withdraw and collect.** `/withdraw` sends pUSD to a bridge address that pays out USDC on Base to the account. `/redeem` collects a resolved market's winnings. Both are wallet batches signed with the passkey.

`/events`, `/up-or-down`, `/markets/[id]`, and `/history` read Polymarket's public data. `/markets/[id]` takes a Gamma market id or a market slug, since Polymarket's positions name their market by slug. Sports and esports are filtered twice: Gamma excludes the Sports tag, then every event and market is checked again for sports and esports tags, game IDs, and team IDs (`isSports`). A sports market answers as not found.

## Screens

Perps and Predictions are two sections in the app's menu (since 3 October 2026; before, one Markets section with two tabs). `/app/perps` is the perps home and `/app/perps/[coin]` a perp's page (a stock perp's coin is URL-encoded, such as `xyz%3ASPCX`, and shown without its dex); `/app/predictions` is the predictions home and `/app/predictions/[id]` a prediction market's page, by Gamma id or slug. The old addresses redirect: `/app/markets` to `/app/perps`, `/app/markets?view=predictions` to `/app/predictions`, and `/app/markets/perps/*` and `/app/markets/predictions/*` (query kept) to the new pages. Components are in `apps/web/src/components/` (`markets-*.ts(x)` shared, `perps-*.tsx`, `prediction*-*.tsx`), pure helpers in `lib/markets/view.ts`, and styles in `src/app/markets.css` (`mk`) and, for predictions, `src/app/predictions.css` (`pd`). Predictions' own client reads are in `components/predictions-data.ts` and its pure helpers in `lib/markets/predictions-view.ts`. End-to-end specs: `perps.spec.ts` and `predictions.spec.ts`.

- **The perps account** shows its value, **available to trade** (each dex's value less the margin in use, `availableToTrade`, as Hyperliquid's order form says it), and **you can withdraw** (Hyperliquid's `withdrawable`, which also keeps back a share of every open position's value, so it can be $0 while there's money to trade). `/api/perps/account` also says what would stop the customer before they start (`blocked`): a locked account (every trade, add, cancel, close, and withdrawal; the action routes refuse them with `409 account_locked`) or a place Hyperliquid doesn't serve (anything new; closing and withdrawing stay open; `451 place_restricted` on the routes). The account card, the order panel, and the phone's Long and Short buttons show its sentence and disable what it stops, so nobody fills in an order to be refused at the end. An empty account (value and withdrawable both $0, as for a new customer) shows Add money alone, as the primary button, with the $6 minimum under it; Withdraw appears once there's money. With the switch on, a new customer's reads answer `200` with a $0 account and empty lists; the `503 feature_unavailable` the quality sweep saw is the switch being off in its database, which the page shows as "Perps aren't available yet".
- **A perp's page** is a trading screen. Desktop: a breadcrumb (Perps / Crypto or Stocks / the market, whose name opens a list to switch market), the price with the day's change in dollars and percent, volume, open interest, and funding (which side pays); the chart (`/api/perps/candles`, ranges Live to All, line or candles; Live polls every 3 seconds); positions, open orders, and history; the order book (`/api/perps/book`, polled every 2.5 seconds, asks above the spread and bids below, each row with a depth bar); and the order panel, always open. Under 1280px the book moves under the chart, and under 1024px everything is one column. Phone: the book is a tab beside positions, and Long and Short open the same form in a sheet.
- **Book grouping.** A dropdown offers dollar steps that suit the price (`bookGroupings`): Hyperliquid rounds to 2 to 5 significant figures, and at 5 steps the last figure by 2 or 5, so BTC at 84,553 offers 1, 2, 5, 10, 100, and 1,000, and the book is read with `sig` and `mantissa`. The first step is the full book. **Picking a price** in the book starts a limit order at it: an ask starts a long (a buy at that price), a bid a short, the side that would trade with that row, as most exchanges do. On the phone it opens the order sheet.
- **The order form** (`PerpsOrderForm` in `perps-order.tsx`, shared by the panel and the sheet): Market or Limit (with a Price now button that fills in the mid price), Cross or Isolated (with a line saying what each risks), Long or Short (each with "Price goes up" or "Price goes down" under it, also on the phone's trade bar), a dollar amount with 25%, 50%, 75%, and Max, leverage by slider or number (with a line that works the chosen leverage through: "at 40x, $10 trades like $400"), and an optional take profit and stop loss. The amount shows what's available to trade in that market (for a stock market, its dex plus the main dex's withdrawable, which the server moves across) and, apart, the Base USDC the same tap can add (all but a dollar, from the $6 minimum); Max counts both (`maxOrderMargin`). Leverage starts at the market's maximum, or at an open position's leverage and margin mode in that market, since a change applies to it. Number fields keep only digits and one decimal point (`cleanDecimal`, `cleanWhole`). Take profit and stop loss are checked against the price for the side and show the estimated profit or loss at each. Position size, margin, estimated fee, and liquidation price come from `/api/perps/trade/preview`; a `null` fee shows as unavailable, and a refused preview shows the server's words. The button says what the tap does: "Add money and long BTC" when the perps balance is short, otherwise "Long BTC". The line under it says what margin and the liquidation price mean and, for a market order, that it trades at most 1% from the price shown. Hyperliquid's refusals are put in plain words with what to do (`orderRefusal`); one it doesn't list keeps Hyperliquid's sentence without the asset number.
- **Positions** show size and value, entry price, the price now, profit with return on margin, liquidation price, margin, and the take profit and stop loss resting on them (reduce-only trigger orders for that market). **Take profit / Stop loss** opens a sheet that sets them on the whole position (`/positions/tpsl`), then cancels the ones of the same kind it replaces, and can remove one. **Close** opens a sheet: market or limit, and 25%, 50%, 75%, or all. All at market uses `/positions/close`; anything else is a reduce-only order through `/orders`, sized down to the market's lot (`closeSize`). Open orders and history use plain words ("Limit buy ETH", "Take profit on BTC", "Opened long").
- **Adding money** (the sheet, and the first step of one tap) finishes when either comes first: the action settles, or the perps account, read every 3 seconds, shows the amount less a dollar (`addToPerps`). The step shows the network fee from the prepared action's review (`fromAmountRaw − toAmountRaw`), without naming the provider; after 90 seconds it says it's slower than usual. The sheet's Close always works: once sent, the money keeps moving and Transactions tracks it. On the phone, closing the order sheet mid-way says that money being added still arrives but the order isn't placed.
- **One tap.** The order form runs every step the customer still needs, in place: if the perps balance is short, a deposit of the shortfall plus $1 (at least the $6 minimum), waited on as above; then setup and its one passkey signature if this device's trading key isn't approved; then the trade. Predictions does the same: each setup step until `ready`, a deposit of the shortfall if pUSD cash is short (raised to the bridge's minimum when it refuses a smaller one), a wait until the cash shows on Polymarket, then the buy, signed with the passkey and posted to `/signatures`. A cancelled passkey stops the steps and says nothing more was sent.
- **Reads.** Each panel shows its source and read time. A failed read shows as unavailable, never as the last value. With a switch off, the API answers `feature_unavailable` and the page says the area isn't available yet.
- **A prediction market's page** is laid out like Polymarket's. Desktop (1024px and up): the question with its volume and end; the odds chart (`/api/predictions/history`, ranges 1H, 6H, 1D, 1W, 1M, All) with the chance now and what it was at the start of the range; the customer's position with Sell, or Collect once it has won; and the rules; with the order panel on the right, always open (`PredictionTradeForm`: Buy or Sell, the outcome with its price in cents, a dollar amount with +$1, +$20, +$100, and Max, or shares with 25%, 50%, and Max to sell, then price, shares, pay from, and To win). Under 1024px the panel moves below. Phone: Buy Yes and Buy No under the chart open the same form in a bottom sheet with a keypad. A closed market says it has ended and, once Polymarket prices one outcome at $1, which one won.
- **Up or Down.** A market tagged `up-or-down` (`upOrDownInfo`) shows the price to beat, the current price and how far it is above or below, the time left (or "Starts in" before the window), and a live chart. The browser streams Chainlink's price straight from Polymarket's real-time data socket (`wss://ws-live-data.polymarket.com`, topic `crypto_prices_chainlink`, filter `{"symbol":"btc/usd"}`; one connection per asset, about a minute of ticks on subscribing, then one a second, a `PING` every 5 seconds). The CSP's `connect-src 'self' https: wss:` already allows it. After three failed connections, or 15 seconds without a tick, the current price shows as unavailable; it keeps retrying every 15 seconds. The price to beat is Polymarket's: Gamma's `eventMetadata.priceToBeat` once a window resolves, and while it runs, the server reads polymarket.com's crypto price API (`upOrDownPriceToBeat`, best effort) for every started window on `/api/predictions/up-or-down` and for the market on `/api/predictions/markets/[id]` (as `upOrDown`). If that read fails, the page uses the stream's tick at the exact second the window started, which is the number Polymarket uses (checked live on 3 October 2026); opened later than about a minute into a window with both missing, it shows as unavailable.
- **Setup, in steps.** The order form and Add money run setup in place as three steps: create the predictions account (Polygon confirms the Deposit Wallet), allow it to trade (passkey), and connect it to Polymarket (passkey; the request names `ClobAuth`). A setup left part way (`connection.status` pending) shows "Finish setup" on the predictions home.
- **Failures in words.** `predictionErrorCopy` maps the server's error codes to plain sentences ("We couldn't reach Polygon, where your predictions account is, just now. Nothing was sent."); an unknown code keeps the server's sentence. An unavailable cash read says why (`chain_unavailable` names Polygon).
- **Guests** see labelled example markets and accounts (`lib/example/markets.ts`) and sign in to trade.
- **Prices.** A buy is priced at the best ask, else the midpoint. The order sheet's liquidation price comes from `/api/perps/trade/preview`, never the browser.

## Configuration

- `MARKETS_CREDENTIAL_KEY` (secret): 32 random bytes, base64.
- `POLYMARKET_BUILDER_API_KEY`, `POLYMARKET_BUILDER_SECRET`, `POLYMARKET_BUILDER_PASSPHRASE` (secrets) and `POLYMARKET_BUILDER_CODE` (variable), from Polymarket's builder settings.

## In Transactions

Money moved between the account and its own perps or predictions account shows in Transactions under **Markets**, with the venue as the counterparty, and counts as moved, not income or spending, in Insights (`lib/activity/entries.ts`):

- **Added to perps** and **Added to predictions** are the deposit actions (their summary's `market` is `hyperliquid` or `polymarket`).
- **Withdrawn from perps** and **Withdrawn from predictions** are the USDC transfers on Base that the withdrawals pay out. Neither comes from an Aura action or from an address that names the venue, so `lib/activity/markets.ts` labels the transfer that fits a recorded withdrawal (`market_operations`, kind `withdraw`): USDC on Base to the address withdrawn to, within a day after it, for the amount less at most $2 of fees, the earliest unlabelled one first. The transfer itself is what the chain shows; a missed match only shows as Received.

## Not done yet

- Polymarket session keys, so an order needs no passkey. They need Polymarket to approve the builder key.
- Live checks on dev with real funds. The migration and secrets are on dev (3 October 2026); the code reaches dev when this merges. Signing was checked against Hyperliquid's live API with throwaway keys (its errors named exactly the signing address), and the CCTP burn was simulated on Base; fills, margin moves, and a real deposit are not yet checked.
- The fee estimate assumes a market order pays the taker rate and that stock markets charge double; a dex in Hyperliquid's growth mode charges less.
- Funding paid on each position isn't shown: `accountState` doesn't read Hyperliquid's `cumFunding` yet.
- The Culture category reads Polymarket's `pop-culture` tag, which isn't yet checked against live Gamma data.
- Limit orders for predictions (Polymarket's Market or Limit) and a market's full rules text (Gamma's `description`) aren't sent to the app yet. The buy quote carries Polymarket's taker fee (`quote.fee`), but the order form doesn't show it yet.
- A page for a multi-outcome event; its card lists every outcome, and each opens its own market.
