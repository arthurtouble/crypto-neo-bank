Last reviewed: 25 September 2026. Describes the source branch, deployed to `aura-dev.aurel-events.workers.dev` (the branch may be newer). Dated plans and release logs are in `docs/archive/`; the refactor plan is in the [codebase audit](codebase-audit-2026-09-25.md).

## Built

- **Public app and sign-in.** Landing page, 10 customer sections, desktop and mobile navigation, and guest browsing with labeled fictional data. Privy sign-in by email or wallet, open to everyone; acceptance of the current terms and privacy notice is recorded per version on first sign-in.
- **Account.** A Privy embedded wallet per customer, upgraded in place with EIP-7702, with the same address on every EVM network ([accounts and custody](../architecture/accounts-and-custody.md)). Privy pays gas. TEE execution, gas sponsorship, and MFA are set in the Privy dashboard.
- **Passkey requirement.** A passkey or authenticator app is required before money leaves. `POST /api/actions`, `POST /api/actions/:id/submit`, and `POST /api/money/payouts` refuse with `mfa_required` without one, and the app opens Privy's enrollment.
- **Money actions** ([money actions](../architecture/money-actions.md)). `POST /api/actions` prepares exact calls, the server builds a `wallet_sendCalls` request, the browser signs a Privy authorization signature, and the server relays it through Privy with `sponsor: true`. It then verifies operation identity, finality, expected events, and cross-chain delivery from chain evidence. Records: `actions`, `action_events`, `route_quotes`.
- **Server checks on every action.** Feature switches, account lock, the optional daily limit, saved-recipients-only mode, and new-recipient cooling. Tightening applies at once. Loosening (unlock, raise or remove the limit, allow any address, shorten the wait) needs a server-verified passkey confirmation (`lib/security/step-up.ts`). Every change is audited. The lock also refuses actions prepared before it, at authorize and submit.
- **Deposit.** The account address and QR code on Base; adding from a connected wallet on Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, or Polygon, moved through LI.FI to the same asset on Base (`POST /api/deposits/quote`, `GET /api/deposits/status`); card funding through Privy's `useFundWallet`. A live Ethereum-to-Base deposit has run on dev. Bank deposits show as coming soon.
- **Send.** Every registered `send` asset (ETH, USDC, EURC, WETH, cbBTC, the ten Coinbase stocks on Base, Tether Gold on Ethereum) to an address, saved recipient, Aura tag, or the customer's own linked wallets, with review and passkey. The customer picks the network: Base, or for ETH and USDC another network through a LI.FI route, fees taken from the amount (`sendDestinations` in the registry). A new address can be saved as a named recipient from Send. The server refuses the account's own address and registered token contracts. Confirmed on dev: 1 USDC on Base, and 2 USDC from Base to Ethereum (LI.FI and Polymer, about 22 minutes, 1.995 USDC delivered). Bank payouts show as coming soon.
- **Asset registry** (`apps/web/src/lib/assets/registry.ts`): decides, server-side, what can be shown, deposited, sent, swapped, or bought. Operators pause assets in the operations app (`/api/ops/assets`). See [assets](../architecture/assets.md).
- **Overview and Transactions.** A chain-read Overview (`GET /api/overview`) of cash, crypto, and earn deposits in US dollars, with a total and per-holding source and observation time. Transactions with money received (Alchemy's transfer index), filters, receipts, a detail page, list and tax-support exports, and a monthly statement that is refused rather than incomplete. Screen data hooks with labelled guest examples ([screen data](../architecture/frontend-data.md)).
- **Swaps and cross-chain moves**, through one LI.FI route module: from any held asset (Base, or Tether Gold on Ethereum) to any catalog asset, same or cross chain. Chainlink reference prices with a 2% gap warning for stocks and gold; server-held 45-second quotes; call target and approval spender pinned to the LI.FI Diamond; 3% price-impact cap; registry assets only ([assets](../architecture/assets.md)); customer slippage of 0.1, 0.5, or 1%; a sent-and-tracked handoff. A live 2 USDC → AAPLc swap was confirmed on dev.
- **Earn on Base**, behind `defi_actions`: Aave V3 supply and withdraw of USDC and WETH, and deposits and withdrawals (an exact amount, or every share) in two Morpho USDC vaults, Steakhouse Prime USDC (Vault V2) and Gauntlet USDC Prime (V1). The server re-checks each vault's asset and V2 access gates on chain before building; rates come from Morpho's public API. Sky savings on Ethereum was cut: it needed USDC on Ethereum, which Aura accounts don't hold.
- **Aura tags.** A unique, non-transferable tag, a verified linked receiving wallet, public opt-in, and a public crypto payment page. Bank details appear only for an activated Bridge customer; card payment is unavailable.
- **Provider projections** for cards and wallet-provider rules, applied from signed provider events by the events Worker ([provider projections](../architecture/provider-projections.md)).
- **Notifications** (`lib/notifications`): a header bell with unread notices, toasts, email through Resend, and browser push (Web Push with VAPID, `public/sw.js`). Triggered by money received (checked on app polls and by the cron), the customer's actions completing or failing, and security changes (lock, loosening, new recipient, closing and reopening). Security notices are always emailed; transaction notices follow the customer's choices.
- **Privacy and closing.** Notification choices, product-update consent, and an instant data download backed by a classified inventory of every customer-data table. No customer deletion: an operator closes an account on request once it holds no funds (`lib/account/closure.ts`, `/api/ops/accounts`), and a closed account is refused everywhere except support and the data download.
- **Support** through Intercom (`lib/support/intercom.ts`, `components/support-chat.tsx`): the Messenger with Fin answering first, identified by a server-signed JWT; Fin reads the customer's latest transactions through a read-only data connector; report-a-problem paths (someone else using the account points to the lock in Settings; money sent to a scam).
- **Insights** (`GET /api/insights`): money in, out, put to work, and moved, for 7, 30, or 90 days or a year; a money in and out chart by day, week, or month (UTC), with a table view; the five card merchants paid most. A total, or one side of the chart, shows as unavailable when received money or card payments can't all be read.
- **Operations app** (`apps/ops`, Worker `aurel-ops`, dev `aura-dev-ops`), separate from the customer app and behind Cloudflare Access. Its Worker forwards `/api/<path>` to the web app's `/api/ops/<path>` over a service binding. The web app checks the operator's Access token on every request (`lib/auth/access.ts`) and records the operator's email on every change. Sections: Customers, Money movement, Stats, and Controls ([operations runbook](../operations/operations-runbook.md#operations-app)). On dev, Cloudflare Access (team `red-surf-47a8`) protects `aura-dev-ops`, and only operators its policy lists can sign in. See [architecture](../architecture/architecture.md#operations-app).
- **Database.** One D1 baseline, `infra/d1/migrations/0001_baseline.sql`, squashed from 42 migrations on 25 September 2026; the dev database was reset to it, and dev deploys reset it again whenever the baseline changes. No production migration or deployment has been done.

## Removed

- **25 September 2026** (see the codebase audit): borrowing, the support assistant, the partner sandbox and demo provider, regulated market orders and eligibility, the portfolio history and tax-lot pipeline, and the previous transaction pipeline (evaluate/prepare/status/reconcile routes, action passkey approval, simulation and fee budgets, the single-DEX swap path, the single-bridge route policy). `/app/borrow` redirects to Earn; swaps go through LI.FI only.
- **Earlier:** the customer waitlist, markets lists, price alerts, goals, scheduled transfers, paycheck planning, external-wallet portfolio tracking, lifestyle concierge, the private-beta invitation gate, and Aura-computed membership tiers. Their tables were dropped (migrations `0040`–`0042`, before the baseline squash) and unredeemed customer referral codes disabled. Audit evidence, consent records, and beta invitations remain.
- **27 September 2026:** Invest; stocks and gold are bought in Swap.
- **28 September 2026:** Rewards, because no provider (Bridge, Stripe) reports memberships or benefits. `/app/rewards`, `GET /api/rewards`, the `membership.updated` and `benefit.entitlement.updated` events, and the `membership_projections` and `benefit_entitlements` tables are gone; their API routes return 404, and `/app/rewards` and `/app/benefits` open Cards. Also: in-house support cases, feedback, Turnstile, the public status and incident routes, the console inside the customer app (`/app/operations`), and `GET /api/ops/analytics`.

## External or incomplete

| Area | Required before customer execution |
| --- | --- |
| Bridge bank deposits and payouts | Built to Bridge's documented API behind `fiat_accounts`, tested end to end against a local Bridge fake: verification, a USD account paying into the Aura account, its details, bank deposits labeled in Transactions, saved banks, payouts funded by a USDC action and followed until Bridge reports the bank has them (or a return). Needs Bridge approval, `BRIDGE_API_KEY`, `BRIDGE_WEBHOOK_PUBLIC_KEY`, a registered webhook, and a sandbox run |
| Bridge + Stripe Issuing cards | Built behind `payment_cards` (phone wallets behind `card_wallets`), tested against local Bridge and Stripe fakes: Bridge card approval, one virtual Visa card per account, spending allowance on Base, freeze and daily limit with passkey step-up, card details in Stripe's frames, card activity, disputes. Card payments, holds, declines, and refunds appear in Transactions, statements, and Insights, from Stripe. Needs an approved Bridge card program, a Stripe account (`STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`), `BRIDGE_CARDS_SPENDER`, a registered Stripe webhook, and a real card on dev. Apple and Google Pay need Stripe preview access |
| Card payments on Aura tag pages | Acquiring or payment-link provider, recipient onboarding, payment state, refunds, and disputes |
| Account and gas sponsorship | Privy dashboard: TEE execution, gas sponsorship (app pays) on every network Aura sends from, and MFA with passkeys or authenticator apps |
| Funded actions | Run on dev: a deposit from Ethereum to Base, a send on Base, a send from Base to Ethereum, a USDC to AAPLc swap, and a Morpho vault deposit and full withdrawal. Every other action kind (Aave, other routes) needs a funded rehearsal before its switch is turned on |
| Syrup (Maple) in Earn | Deferred: deposits need Maple's per-wallet authorization, and withdrawals queue for up to 30 days |
| Tokenized stocks and metals | Registered and valued with Chainlink feeds; held, sent, and traded in Swap (a funded USDC to AAPLc swap was checked on dev). No separate Invest screen |
| Card cashback | Cut with Rewards. May return as its own feature once cards are live, with a funded provider program |
| Passcode and remote sessions | Supported identity-provider controls and recovery process |
| Notifications | Built on dev: in the app, email (Resend), and browser push. Production needs a verified Resend sending domain (until then Resend only sends to the Resend account owner's address), `EMAIL_FROM`, `APP_ORIGIN`, and its own VAPID key pair |
| Production release | Secrets, reviewed migration, security and marketing approvals, provider contracts, domain controls, staffed operations, and final smoke checks |

Providers, chains, and protocols are authoritative for balances and settlement; D1 projections never are. Marketing copy is pending the registered human claim review. User approval of the implementation plan is not legal or marketing signoff.
