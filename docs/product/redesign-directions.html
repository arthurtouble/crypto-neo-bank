Aura redesign: three directions

Parked until the journeys are locked. Quick mockups of Overview and Send, to pick a look after the product skeleton is settled. Same fictional example data in each. Desktop and phone share a theme but not a layout: each has its own navigation, components, and flow. Not final screens.

Screen
Mock theme

A · Console

exact · quiet · fast

A clean break from today. White and cool grey, one blue for actions, an icon rail instead of a sidebar. Home is one holdings table you filter, with recent activity beside it. Add, Send, and Swap open in one drawer over whatever you're looking at, so moving money never loses your place.

Desktop

Icon rail, ⌘K, tables, and a Move money drawer with the form and review together.

Phone

Bottom tabs with Move in the middle. Send is full-screen steps: amount on a keypad with recent people above it, then who, then review.

References

  • Linear: speed, a command bar, nothing extra
  • Mercury: calm tables, exact numbers
  • Stripe Dashboard: drawers for actions, clear hierarchy

What makes it fast

  • One Move entry for Add, Send, and Swap, with amount first
  • Review sits under the form, so there's no separate review screen
  • ⌘K search reaches every page and action
  • Holdings in one table with filters instead of five panels

Risk

Plain if the type and spacing aren't precise. Needs strong empty and loading states to feel finished.

B · Instrument

precise · fast · transparent

Dark first, dense, keyboard-led. A top bar with every section, tables with column headers, one cool blue for actions. Every figure shows its source and time. For people who check their money often and want everything on one screen.

Desktop

Top tabs, dense tables, form and review side by side.

Phone

Bottom tabs, a two-column summary strip, two-column rows, and the same two-step form stacked.

References

  • Linear: speed, keyboard, restraint
  • Kraken Pro and Bloomberg: dense, exact tables
  • Vercel dashboard: small type, strong grid

What makes it fast

  • Every section one click away in the top bar; ⌘K for everything else
  • Form and review side by side, both visible before you confirm
  • Source and time on every figure, so nothing needs a second look

Risk

Can read as a trading terminal, which the design system says to avoid. Dense on a phone.

Desktop, 1280 wide
Phone, 390 wide

C · Pocket

warm · direct · confident

Phone first. One big balance on a colour block, four quick actions under it, a bottom tab bar on phones. People and assets get round avatars; amounts are large and centred when you enter them. Built to become a native app later.

Desktop

A light sidebar, the balance block, and two columns of rounded panels.

Phone

Bottom tabs, quick actions under the balance, centred amount entry, and people as avatars.

References

  • Revolut and Monzo: balance first, quick actions
  • Cash App: big amount entry, people first
  • Wise: plain words about fees and timing

What makes it fast

  • Balance and the four main actions in the first screen, one thumb away
  • Amount first, then who: the order people think in
  • Saved people and your own wallets as one-tap avatars

Risk

Furthest from today. More colour and larger surfaces need care to stay clear of the generated-app look.

Desktop, 1280 wide
Phone, 390 wide
