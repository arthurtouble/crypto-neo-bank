Aura design system

Tokens, components, and patterns for Aura on desktop and phone, in light and dark. Calm and exact: neutral greys, one ultramarine accent, Geist with tabular figures, balanced density, soft corners. Every value comes from design-tokens.css; the rules are in DESIGN.md and docs/product/design-system.md.

Principles

  • Information first, actions second, decoration never. A border, fill, or shadow has to mean something: a group, an object, or something that floats.
  • One accent. Ultramarine marks the primary action, the current place in the navigation, links, selection, and focus. Nothing else is blue.
  • Green, red, and amber mean completed, failed, and needs attention. They're never decoration.
  • Money is exact: tabular figures, currency and asset always shown, and unavailable values say Unavailable in amber, never a stale number or zero.
  • One primary action per screen or panel.
  • Desktop and phone share tokens but not layouts: a sidebar, side panels, and tables on desktop; a menu button, full-screen steps, and lists on the phone.
  • Every screen has loading, empty, error, unavailable, disabled, and blocked states, in light and dark.

Colour

Semantic tokens only; components never use a literal colour. Values below are for the theme shown now.

Pairings

  • Body text: text on canvas, surface, or raised. Secondary text: text-secondary on the same.
  • Tertiary text is for timestamps and hints only, and never on muted.
  • Buttons: on-accent on accent. Tinted notes: the status colour's text on its soft background.
  • All pairings meet WCAG AA (4.5:1) in both themes.

Type

Geist for everything, Geist Mono for addresses, hashes, and references. Three weights: 400, 500, 600. Tabular figures everywhere. Phone sizes in brackets.
