## Documentation rules

- Update the relevant page in the same change as the product or operating process.
- Record dates, owners, evidence, and review deadlines for decisions and launch gates.
- Keep customer-facing explanations in `apps/docs`; keep internal procedures and analysis here.
- Do not store credentials, recovery material, private keys, customer identity documents, or raw provider secrets in Markdown.
- Treat a checked box as a claim that requires evidence, not as a planning preference.
