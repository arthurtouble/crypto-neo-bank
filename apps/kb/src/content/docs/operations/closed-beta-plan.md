---
title: Closed beta plan
description: Cohorts, controls, success measures, stop conditions, and expansion decisions.
---

## Purpose

Prove that customers can safely understand, fund, use and recover the product before broad distribution. The beta excludes fiat accounts, conversion and cards until a regulated provider is contracted and reconciled end to end.

## Cohorts

### Cohort 0 — internal acceptance

- 5 testers, including at least 2 people unfamiliar with crypto workflows.
- Complete authentication, recovery, receiving, small Base USDC send, Aave preview/action where suitable, cross-chain quote/action where suitable, activity verification and support intake.
- No expansion while any critical/high security issue or unexplained financial-state discrepancy exists.

### Cohort 1 — friendly beta

- Up to 25 invited users.
- Manual invitation and named support contact.
- Default USD 25,000 rolling 24-hour preparation limit; USD 1,000 new-destination cooling threshold.
- Weekly review of activation, failed actions, stale transactions, support, incidents and retention.

### Cohort 2 — controlled external beta

- Up to 100 approved users in counsel/provider-approved countries.
- Opens only after Cohort 1 meets every exit gate for two consecutive weeks.
- Limits can be lowered by risk; increases require documented review, never merely a tier badge.

## Success measures

- 90% of invited activated users complete setup without staff intervention.
- At least 98% completion for supported test flows, excluding customer rejection and documented upstream outage.
- 100% of Aurel-created transactions have an instruction, policy result, state history and source evidence.
- No unexplained projection-versus-chain/provider break remains open beyond one business day.
- Urgent cases acknowledged within 15 minutes during published coverage; normal cases within one business day.
- No critical/high unresolved security finding, misleading product claim or unsupported jurisdiction exposure.

## Stop conditions

Pause the affected path immediately for signer ambiguity, incorrect destination/amount, authentication boundary failure, provider event replay, unexplained balance/settlement discrepancy, data exposure, sanctions instruction, repeated routing failure or inability to provide incident coverage. Use the server-side account/product lock and provider disable flags; do not rely on UI copy alone.

## Daily operating review

- Scheduled reconciliation result and open critical/high issues.
- Submitted transactions older than 15 minutes.
- Failed/dead-letter provider events and replay evidence.
- Support queue, suspected account compromise and complaint escalation.
- Upstream status, quote failure rate and customer-visible degradation.
- Deployment/version changes and rollback readiness.

## Expansion decision

Expansion requires written sign-off from product/security, operations/support and legal/compliance owners. Attach acceptance evidence, unresolved exceptions, current limits, current disclosures and the rollback/disable test. User count alone is not a reason to expand.
