This page is historical. Aura retired public waitlist intake, public growth events, referrals, campaigns, and experiments. Their routes no longer accept creation. The original [growth specification](/growth/marketing-growth-implementation-spec/) records the earlier design; it does not describe current product behavior.

Authorized operators may review retained waitlist and invitation records for access evidence and data requests. A country hint inferred from an IP address is not verified residence or eligibility. Invitations are created through the separate protected invitation controls, shown once, and sent through an approved contact process. Existing invitation, suspension, country, and feature controls still apply.

`docs/operations/aura-production-release-plan.md` records current product availability and release gates. Historical records and financial or security evidence must follow their approved retention rules.
