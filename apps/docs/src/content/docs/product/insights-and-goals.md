## Insights

Insights summarize completed actions recorded in Aurel Activity. Choose 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or one year.

Aurel separates actions by what they actually did:

- **Money Out** includes completed sends, transfers, withdrawals, and repayments.
- **Money In** includes completed borrowing recorded by Aurel.
- **Put to Work** includes completed Earn allocations.
- **Moved** includes swaps and cross-network routes.

Pending, failed, cancelled, and approval-required actions are excluded. Aurel uses a recorded dollar estimate when one exists. USD and USDC amounts can be read directly; Aurel does not guess a historical dollar value for other assets.

Insights are useful for orientation, not bookkeeping. Provider statements and blockchain records remain authoritative. The Activity export is not a bank statement or tax report.

## Goals

A goal is a name, target amount, asset, and optional target date. Creating one does not move, lock, or reserve money.

Until a provider account or onchain subaccount is linked, Aurel shows the target without a progress balance. This avoids presenting a local database number as money you own.

You can pause and resume a goal. A future linked-source version can calculate verified progress from a provider or chain without turning Aurel's database into a ledger.

## What Aurel stores

Aurel stores the goal definition as product-planning data. It does not store an authoritative current amount, accrued interest, or settlement state. Goal records can be rebuilt without affecting customer assets.
