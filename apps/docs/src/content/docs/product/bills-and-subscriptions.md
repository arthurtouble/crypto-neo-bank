title: Bills and subscriptions
description: Track recurring due dates without confusing reminders with payment authority.

## Bill reminders

You can add a bill name, category, optional expected amount, currency, frequency, and next due date. A reminder helps you plan; it does not reserve funds, create a transfer, or authorize payment.

You can pause and resume reminders. Aurel records those changes for account history.

## Observed subscriptions

After a supported bank or card provider is connected, recurring merchants can appear as observed subscriptions. Their merchant name, expected amount, cadence, and next expected date come from that provider.

An observed subscription is not necessarily a contractual subscription, and it is not proof that a merchant will charge again. Aurel labels uncertain provider observations instead of converting them into payment instructions.

## Automatic payments

Automatic payments are not live. They require a provider-backed mandate, identity and eligibility checks, clear cancellation rules, and provider-confirmed payment states.

Aurel will not turn a reminder into autopay. When automatic payments become available, setting one up will be a separate review and confirmation flow.
