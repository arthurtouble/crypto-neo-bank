Aurel membership is designed around the depth and duration of a customer relationship. It does not use an Aurel token.

## Projected tiers

Until Aurel has 30 days of eligible balance history, the product can only show a projection based on currently visible eligible USDC. A projection is not an earned tier.

Relationship duration matters because a momentary balance is easy to move in solely to claim a benefit. The final calculation should use transparent eligible balances, observation periods, and benefit dates rather than an opaque engagement score.

Membership is not a recommendation to hold more stablecoins or DeFi exposure than is suitable. A customer should not borrow, delay a necessary withdrawal, or accept unwanted protocol risk to reach a tier.

## Planned benefits

The product model includes travel support, insurance, eSIMs, subscriptions, cashback, and concierge services. None of these is active until Aurel contracts the provider, defines eligibility, publishes the terms, funds the benefit, and owns the support path.

When benefits launch, the product will show:

- who supplies the benefit;
- who can use it and where;
- the allowance and renewal period;
- exclusions and claim steps; and
- what happens when a tier changes.

Benefits should reward an enduring relationship without pressuring a customer to keep unsuitable assets or take more risk.

## Benefit states

- **Concept:** shown only in roadmap or explanatory material.
- **Projected:** the customer appears likely to qualify if the product and history requirements are met.
- **Available:** the provider, eligibility, geography, allowance, and support path are active.
- **Used:** the provider accepted or fulfilled the benefit.
- **Expired:** the allowance period ended under published terms.

A tier label alone does not move a benefit into the available state.

## Tier changes

Future terms should explain the measurement window, grace period, when upgrades take effect, when downgrades take effect, how pending claims are handled, and whether a paid membership is separate from balance-based recognition.

Removing value without warning would damage trust. Granting benefits instantly against a temporary balance can make the economics unsustainable. A measured history and clear renewal period balance both concerns.

## Benefit economics

Every benefit needs a funded cost model. Card rewards may be supported by interchange; lounge access may be charged per visit; insurance may require a group policy and eligibility rules; concierge requests create staffing or vendor costs.

Aurel should cap cost before promising a benefit, show material exclusions, and avoid describing discount inventory as a cash-equivalent reward.

## Core product is not tiered safety

Account lock, transaction review, security support, accurate disclosures, and access to customer records are not luxury benefits. Membership can change service level or funded extras, not the baseline duty to operate safely.
