---
title: Add money
description: "Four ways to add money to your Aura account on Base: receive crypto at your address, move it from a wallet on Base or another network, buy USDC by card, or, once live, a bank transfer."
---

Open **Add money** and pick a way. Whatever you add ends up in your Aura account on Base.

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Receive** | Shows your account address and QR code, for an exchange or another wallet to send to on Base |
| **From a wallet** | Moves money from a wallet you connected, on Base or another network |
| **Card** | Buys USDC with a debit or credit card |
| **Bank** | US bank details for a bank transfer. [Not available yet](/product/bank-transfers/) |

## Receive

Pick what you're sending from the list, then copy your address or scan the QR code. On a phone, the address shows its first and last four characters; compare those with what the other app shows. When you send, choose Base as the network, and send only an asset in the list. Money sent on another network, or an asset that isn't listed, won't show in Aura. Send a small amount first and check it arrives.

## From a wallet

Connect your wallet, pick the network and asset, and enter an amount, or select **Max** to add all of a token. From Base, you can add ETH, USDC, or EURC. From Ethereum, Arbitrum, or Optimism, you can add ETH or USDC, and from Polygon, USDC. From another network, Aura moves it to the same asset on Base.

The review shows about how much you'll get, the least you'll get, and the fees: their total, with the moving fee and the network fee underneath. Your wallet pays the network fee on the network you send from, and the moving fee comes out of the amount. Select **Add from wallet** to confirm, then confirm in your wallet. A move from another network usually arrives in a few minutes. You can add more while it travels, and **Transactions** shows it as pending until it arrives, even if you leave Add money. If it can't reach Base, the money goes back to your wallet, and Transactions shows it as failed.

## Card

Select **Pay by card**. Privy, which also runs Aura's sign-in, opens a checkout where its card partner charges your card. Before you pay, the checkout shows the partner's name, its fee, its limits, and how much USDC you get. Aura doesn't add a fee. The USDC arrives in your account on Base. If Card says card payments aren't available, use one of the other ways.

Aura can't recover assets sent on the wrong network or in the wrong token. Check the network and token contract first; see [before you send to your Aura account](/product/networks-and-assets/#before-you-send-to-your-aura-account).
