---
title: Concierge and support
description: What Aurel support and the read-only concierge can do—and what they cannot do.
---

Aurel combines product guidance, account support, and a read-only financial concierge. None of these channels can sign a transaction or take control of a wallet.

## Product concierge

The concierge can explain Aurel features, surface relevant documentation, compare visible product choices, summarize general risks, and help a customer find the correct workflow.

It cannot:

- sign, submit, or approve a transaction;
- change security controls or destination rules;
- move funds or recover a private key;
- guarantee a return, route, benefit, or settlement time;
- provide individualized legal, tax, or regulated investment advice;
- override eligibility or provider decisions.

AI-generated explanations can be wrong or incomplete. Material transaction details come from the transaction preview, wallet request, chain, protocol, and provider—not from conversational text.

## Support cases

Authenticated customers can open normal or urgent cases. To reduce automated abuse, case submission is rate limited. An urgent label helps route an issue; it does not create emergency custody, transaction reversal, or guaranteed recovery.

Useful information includes:

- the affected feature and network;
- a public transaction hash or route reference;
- the approximate time of the action;
- the state shown in Aurel;
- screenshots with personal information removed where possible.

Do not send private keys, seed phrases, recovery secrets, full identity documents, or one-time authentication codes.

## Response targets

The current operating targets are a 15-minute acknowledgement for urgent cases during coverage and one business day for a first response to normal cases. These are launch objectives, not contractual service-level guarantees.

## Security reports

Suspected vulnerabilities should use the process in [Report a security issue](/safety/report-a-security-issue/). A vulnerability report is different from a customer-support case and may require coordinated disclosure.

## Complaints

A service complaint should include the relevant account reference, dates, affected service, what happened, and the outcome requested. Aurel records the complaint and, where another provider owns the service, routes it without suggesting that responsibility has disappeared. See the [Complaints policy](/legal/complaints/).

## Future private-client service

Higher membership levels may include faster routing, named support, travel assistance, or access to third-party benefits. Any such benefit depends on published eligibility, a contracted fulfilment provider, geographic availability, and benefit-specific terms. Core security support is not withheld from lower membership levels.
