---
title: Lost access and recovery
description: How to get back into your Aura account and wallet through Privy sign-in, what Aura can and can't restore, and how to prepare before you lose access.
---

Privy, an independent company, runs sign-in and secures the key for your wallet. Your wallet is your Aura account, so getting back in means signing in to Privy again.

## Getting back in

Sign in with any method linked to your account: your email, Google, Telegram, or the wallet you signed up with. You get the same account and the same address.

Aura can't restore a lost sign-in. We don't hold your key, and support can't sign in for you, reset your sign-in, or move your money.

## If someone else may have access

1. If you can still sign in, turn on the emergency lock in **Settings → Security**. Nothing can be sent through Aura while it's on.
2. Secure your email, the wallet you sign in with, and the device that holds your passkey.
3. Tell us in the chat from **Support → Report a problem**.

Our team can also lock your account to protect it. Only you can unlock it, in Settings with your passkey. The lock only covers Aura: it can't stop someone who has your exported key and uses it in another app.

## Prepare now

- Keep a second way to sign in. Every account has an email; if yours came from Google, you can add another in **Settings → Security**.
- Sign in with each method once, while your balance is small.
- Keep your passkey device, email, and sign-in wallet secure.
- Understand what exporting your wallet key means before you do it: anyone with the key can move your money, outside Aura's controls.
- Keep your public address separate from anything you use to recover access.
