Aura's support is a chat inside the app. Open **Support** and select **Chat**. You need to be signed in.

Aura's assistant answers straight away and can look at your latest transactions. Our team takes over when it can't help. Intercom runs the chat and keeps the conversation.

If the chat can't load, for example because a content blocker stops it, Support says so and offers **Try again**. If your account can't load at all, that screen has **Chat with support**.

Never share a seed phrase, private key, recovery secret, or one-time code, in the chat or anywhere else. We'll never ask for one.

## Report a problem

**Support → Report a problem** has two shortcuts:

- **Someone else may be using my account.** Turn on the emergency lock in Settings first, so nothing can be sent. Then tell us what happened in the chat.
- **I sent money to a scam or the wrong address.** Tell us which transaction. Crypto transfers can't be reversed by Aura or anyone else, but we'll help you report it.

## Other requests

- **Close your account:** move your money out, then ask in the chat. Once it's closed, Support is the one page you can still open, with the chat and the help articles. See [settings and notifications](/product/settings-and-notifications/#your-data).
- **Complaints:** see the [complaints policy](/legal/complaints/).
- **Privacy requests:** say your request is about privacy. See the [privacy notice](/legal/privacy-notice/).
- **Security vulnerabilities:** see [report a security issue](/safety/report-a-security-issue/).

Can't sign in? See [lost access and recovery](/help/lost-access/).
