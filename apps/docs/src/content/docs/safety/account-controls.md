You can turn on a few optional controls. Making a control stricter takes effect right away. Loosening one, such as unlocking your account or raising your limit, needs your passkey first. That way, someone who gets into your session can't undo them. We keep a record of every change.

The controls are in **Settings → Security**. Settings has six areas: Security, Saved recipients, Aura tag, Notifications, This device, and Your data. On a computer, the areas are listed down the side. On a phone, tap an area to open it.

## Your controls

| Control | Starts as | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency lock | Off | Stops every send, swap, and Earn move, including ones you started but haven't confirmed |
| Daily limit | Off | Caps the US dollar value you send in any 24 hours, including to your own linked wallets |
| Saved recipients only | Off | Lets you send only to recipients you've saved |
| Wait before new recipients | 4 hours | When saved recipients only is on, a newly saved recipient can't receive until the wait ends |

## Passkey

You need a passkey or an authenticator app before money can leave your account. The two work the same way. Add one in **Settings → Security**. If you haven't, we ask you to add one the first time you move money.

You confirm each money action with it, and each change that loosens a control. Unlike the controls above, it isn't optional if you want to move money.

## Emergency lock

Turn on the lock if something looks wrong. For example, an unexpected prompt, a recipient you don't recognize, or signs that someone else has access. Then secure your sign-in methods too. If you think someone else has access, lock your account in **Settings → Security** first. Then tell us from **Support → Report a problem**.

While the lock is on, we don't prepare or send any money movement. That includes one you started but haven't confirmed yet. The lock doesn't freeze your wallet, reverse a transaction already sent, or stop activity in another app. Unlocking needs your passkey.

Once cards are live, locking also tries to freeze your Aura card. You can't unfreeze the card while you're locked. Check the Cards page to make sure it's frozen.

### If we lock your account

Our team can also lock your account to protect it, for example if it looks like someone else is using it. It works the same as your own lock. We tell you by email and in the app. Only you can unlock it, in **Settings** with your passkey. Our team can't unlock it for you.

## Daily limit

The daily limit counts every send, including sends to your own linked wallets. It also counts swaps that pay someone else.

It doesn't count swaps within your own account, or Earn deposits and withdrawals. Setting a card allowance doesn't count either, because the card has its own daily limit. See [cards and controls](/product/cards-and-controls/).

If you've set a limit and we can't work out the value of an amount, we block the action rather than skip the check. Prices move, so the dollar value is approximate.

## Saved recipients

The 4-hour wait for a newly saved recipient only applies when saved recipients only is on.

Give recipients you trust clear names. Before you save an address, check the full address in a separate way. A name helps you recognize someone. It doesn't prove who owns the address.

## When Aura switches a feature off

We can also switch a feature off for everyone, for example sends, swaps, or Earn. While a feature is off, we don't prepare new actions for it. Your wallet still works in other apps.

## Closing your account

Move your money out first. Then contact support from **Settings → Your data**. We close an account once it holds nothing and nothing is in progress.

A closed account can't move money, and we take down its Aura tag. You can still download your data and contact support. We keep your transaction, security, and consent records, as the law and account safety require.

## What controls can't do

Controls only apply when we prepare a money movement. They don't cover network fees, market moves, an exported key, or transactions you make in another app.

## A simple routine

1. Start with a small amount.
2. Check the network and the full address.
3. Read the full review before you confirm.
4. Check the transaction on a block explorer.
5. If a prompt surprises you, cancel it.
