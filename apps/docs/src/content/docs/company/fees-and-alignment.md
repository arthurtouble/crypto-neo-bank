Aura doesn't charge a fee today, including on perps and predictions. Aura could add a fee to swaps; it's set to zero. If that changes, the fee will appear in the quote before you confirm.

## Costs you may still pay

| Cost | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Network fee | The blockchain. Aura pays it for actions from your account, except a deposit to perps, where a few cents come out of the amount. When you add money from your own wallet, that wallet pays it |
| Exchange and moving fees | The exchanges and transfer services that carry out a swap or a move to another network. They come out of the amount, and the review shows them under **Fees** before you confirm |
| Card fee | Privy's card partner, when you add money by card. Its checkout shows the fee before you pay |
| Price changes | The price can move before a swap goes through, and a large amount can get a worse price. You choose how far it can move, and the quote shows the least you'll get |
| Trading fees and funding | Hyperliquid and Polymarket charge their own trading fees. An open perps position also pays or receives funding every hour. A perps order shows Hyperliquid's estimated fee before you confirm |
| Partner fee | A future bank or card partner |

Aura shows the costs it knows about before you confirm. Some are estimates and can change before the transaction goes through.

## How Aura may earn money later

We may use a mix of:

- a paid membership, with clear pricing;
- a disclosed share of card fees paid by merchants;
- disclosed conversion or service fees;
- subscriptions for families or businesses;
- fees for premium support or reporting.

Before we turn any of these on, we'll tell you the amount or how it's calculated, and any conflict of interest. Each one also needs a review of its legal treatment, value to you, refunds, and country availability.

We'll avoid hidden spreads, quietly skimming yield, printing tokens, and dressing up referral payments as independent advice. Fees based on how much you hold can push a company to grow balances when that isn't right for you. If we ever use them, we'll explain why.

## No Aura token

Aura has no token of its own, so no asset's price depends on you staying in the app. An asset or partner should be in Aura because it's useful, understandable, liquid enough, reliable, and suitable for you, not to create demand for something we own.

## No rewards

Aura doesn't offer cashback, points, or other rewards. Someone always pays for perks, so each one would first need a funded partner, limits, eligibility and country rules, and someone responsible for support.

## When fees change

Before a fee change applies, we'll show you the amount or method, when it starts, which service it affects, and your alternatives. Costs for a specific transaction always appear in its quote or review.
