title: Provider responsibilities
description: What Aurel, Privy, protocols, and future regulated partners each do.

  order: 2
---

| Party | Main role | Does not replace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aurel | Product experience, policy checks, records, support routing, and provider oversight | Customer judgment or provider regulation |
| Privy | Authentication and wallet infrastructure | Aurel’s product, security, and disclosure duties |
| Base and protocols | Onchain execution and source data | Aurel support or legal review |
| LI.FI | Route discovery and execution data | Destination confirmation or Aurel route checks |
| Future regulated partners | Their contracted accounts, identity checks, sanctions controls, and regulated records | Aurel’s marketing, access control, security, complaints, and vendor oversight |

A provider can perform customer checks without taking responsibility for everything Aurel says or does. Exact duties must be set in contracts and procedures before a regulated feature launches.
