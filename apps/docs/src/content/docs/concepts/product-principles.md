Aura is a money app built around stablecoins and your own wallet. You can send, earn, and swap between crypto, tokenized stocks, and gold. Once partners are approved, Aura will add bank transfers and cards.

## One app, many systems

Blockchains, wallet software, DeFi protocols, routing services, and future partners each do a different job. Aura puts them in one app without hiding where your money sits, who controls it, or what can go wrong.

Similar-looking balances can behave very differently. A balance may sit in your wallet, in a lending protocol, or, later, with a bank partner. Each has different rights, liquidity, protection, and settlement. Aura shows those differences instead of hiding them in one total.

## No house token

Aura has no token of its own, so no asset's price depends on you staying in the app. An asset or partner earns its place by being useful, well understood, liquid, and reliable.

Aura may later earn from subscriptions, card fees, disclosed partner revenue, or service fees. We'll show you those. They shouldn't decide what we put in front of you.

## Your wallet, your approval

Aura prepares each action and you approve it with your passkey. Aura holds no key that would let an employee or an automated system move your assets.

That doesn't make loss impossible. You could still sign a harmful transaction, lose access to your sign-in, use an exported key elsewhere, or use a protocol that fails. Aura's controls reduce mistakes inside Aura, not outside it.

## Safety you can see

Safety is part of the main screens, not buried in settings:

- a clear review of each transaction before you confirm;
- saved recipients, with an optional saved-only mode and a wait before new ones;
- an optional daily limit and account lock;
- honest labels for what's live, in preview, or unavailable;
- a record of every transaction and the checks Aura ran;
- plain explanations of what each partner and protocol does.

## Useful from the first deposit

You don't need a large balance. You should be able to sign in, see your assets, understand how your account is protected, and try a small transaction first. Basic safety and support are for everyone, not a premium extra.

## Calm, not simplistic

Aura cuts words and choices you don't need, not facts you do. It feels calm because information is well ordered, not because fees, risks, or responsibilities are hidden.
