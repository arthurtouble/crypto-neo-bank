Aura is a money app built around stablecoins and your own wallet. You can send, earn, and swap between crypto, stocks, and gold, and trade perps and predictions from accounts your wallet owns. Once partners are approved, Aura will add bank transfers and cards.

## One app, many systems

Blockchains, wallet software, services like Aave, Morpho, Hyperliquid, and Polymarket, and future partners each do a different job. Aura puts them in one app without hiding where your money sits, who controls it, or what can go wrong.

Similar-looking balances can behave very differently. A balance may sit in your wallet, with Aave or Morpho, in your perps or predictions account, or, later, with a bank partner. Each has different rights, protection, and rules for when you can take money out and how long it takes. Aura shows those differences instead of hiding them in one total.

## No house token

Aura has no token of its own, and how we may earn money later is disclosed first. See [fees](/company/fees-and-alignment/).

## Your wallet, your approval

Aura prepares each action and you approve it with your passkey. Aura holds no key that would let an employee or an automated system move your assets.

That doesn't make loss impossible. You could still sign a harmful transaction, lose access to your sign-in, use an exported key elsewhere, or use a service that fails. Aura's controls reduce mistakes inside Aura, not outside it.

## Safety you can see

Safety is part of the main screens, not buried in settings: a clear review before you confirm, optional [account controls](/safety/account-controls/), honest labels for what's live or unavailable, a record of every transaction and the checks Aura ran, and plain explanations of what each partner does.

## Useful from the first dollar

You don't need a large balance. You should be able to sign in, see your assets, understand how your account is protected, and try a small transaction first. Basic safety and support are for everyone, not a premium extra.

## Calm, not simplistic

Aura cuts words and choices you don't need, not facts you do. It feels calm because information is well ordered, not because fees, risks, or responsibilities are hidden.
