## The essentials

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Try it first" icon="open-book">Every screen works with example data, clearly labeled. No account needed.</Card>
  <Card title="Only you can move your money" icon="key">Aura never holds your keys or your money. Privy secures the key for your wallet, and your passkey confirms every payment.</Card>
  <Card title="Balances from the source" icon="database">Read from the blockchains and protocols that hold your money, never from Aura's own records.</Card>
  <Card title="Check before you confirm" icon="warning">Payments are real and usually final. Check the network, address, asset, and amount.</Card>
</CardGrid>
