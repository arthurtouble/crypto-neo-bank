## The essentials

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Look around first" icon="open-book">Browse Aura with labeled example data before you sign in.</Card>
  <Card title="You hold the keys" icon="key">Your account is your own wallet. Aura never holds your funds or keys. You approve every transaction with your passkey.</Card>
  <Card title="Balances come from the source" icon="database">Balances come from the blockchain and the protocols you use, never from Aura's own records.</Card>
  <Card title="Real money, real networks" icon="warning">Check the network, address, asset, and amount before you confirm.</Card>
</CardGrid>
