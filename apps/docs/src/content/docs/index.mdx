## Start with the essentials

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="You control the wallet" icon="key">
    Aurel prepares actions. Your wallet shows the final request, and you choose whether to sign.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Balances come from the source" icon="database">
    Aurel reads balances from the chain or provider. Our database is never the final record of what you own.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No Aurel token" icon="seti:lock">
    Product choices are not designed to create demand for a house asset.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mainnet means real value" icon="warning">
    Aurel uses Base mainnet. Check the network, address, asset, and amount before every signature.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## What you can do

- View Base assets held by your embedded or connected wallet.
- Send supported assets after a policy and simulation check.
- Review live Aave markets and prepare supply, withdrawal, borrow, or repay actions.
- Request a cross-chain USDC route when LI.FI can find one.
- Set address, review, and account-lock preferences.

Bank accounts, payment cards, tokenized securities, insurance, and fulfilled travel benefits are not live. [See current product status](/getting-started/status/).

:::caution[Real transactions]
Aurel operates on public mainnets. A signed transaction can move real assets and may not be reversible.
:::
