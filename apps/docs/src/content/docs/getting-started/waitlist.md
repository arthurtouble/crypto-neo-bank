title: Join the waitlist
description: How the Aurel private-beta waitlist works.
---

The waitlist is open to people worldwide. It asks for one thing: your email address. Joining does not create an account, reserve a place, or guarantee an invitation.

## What happens after you join

We record your email so we can contact you if access becomes available. We may also record an approximate country hint supplied by our hosting provider and limited campaign labels from the link you used. The country hint is not proof of where you live and cannot qualify you for product access.

An authorized operator checks eligibility separately before issuing a one-use invitation. The private beta remains limited to enabled countries, supported features, and available capacity. We do not send an automatic confirmation email from the waitlist form.

## Your data

Your email is encrypted for storage and indexed with a keyed value to prevent duplicate entries. Submitting the same email again has the same public result. Do not send wallet addresses, account numbers, recovery phrases, or identity documents through the waitlist.

The [privacy notice](/legal/privacy-notice/) is still a pre-launch draft. The waitlist remains closed until the final controller details, retention period, and contact route for data requests are published.
