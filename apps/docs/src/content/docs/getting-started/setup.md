## Explore

Open Aura to browse Overview, Deposit, Send, Swap, Earn, Invest, Cards, Rewards, Transactions, Insights, Settings, and Support. All values in the signed-out view are fictional examples. You can browse without creating an account.

## Sign in

Select **Get Started** and choose a sign-in method offered by Privy. Aura verifies the session before showing personal data. Financial actions also depend on feature availability, your account controls, and your confirmation in the wallet. Use a sign-in method you can recover.

## Your wallet

Your Aura account is a smart wallet on Base, controlled by your Privy sign-in. Its address is your deposit address. Once fee sponsorship is set up, Aura covers network fees on Base for actions it prepares; that setup is not finished yet. Aura cannot recreate a lost sign-in or key. Review recovery and export in Settings.

Aura reads supported assets on Base mainnet. Mainnet uses real assets. Confirm the network, token contract, destination, and amount before sending. An unsupported token may reach an address without appearing in the portfolio. Provider and chain records, rather than Aura's projections, are authoritative for balances and transactions.

## Receive or send

Use Deposit for a crypto address or public Aura tag page. Bank instructions appear only when Bridge provides complete verified details. Use Send for supported crypto transfers and saved recipients. Read the wallet prompt before signing. A signed transaction may be irreversible. If a transfer appears delayed, check the source transaction and destination before trying again.

Creating an Aura account does not create a bank account or payment card, activate rewards, or qualify you for securities. [Check current availability](/getting-started/status/).
