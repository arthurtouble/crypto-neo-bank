description: What Aurel asks for, how applications are reviewed, and what happens next.
---

Aurel opens in small country and capacity cohorts. An application helps us understand the job you need to do; it does not create a product account, reserve a place, or ask you to deposit money.

## What we ask for

The form asks for your email, country, primary goal, how often that need occurs, a broad description of your current setup, and the outcome you want. An asset range is optional. Do not submit account numbers, wallet addresses, identity documents, recovery phrases, or transaction credentials.

Operational contact about the application is required so we can review it. Product and trust updates are a separate, optional choice. Declining marketing does not affect review.

## Review and invitations

An authorized operator reviews applications individually. Aurel does not automatically qualify someone from their country, asset range, or wallet activity. If the cohort is a fit and the country is enabled, the operator may issue a one-use invitation. Provider and product eligibility checks still apply after invitation.

Referral applicants use the same form and review path. A referral does not bypass geography, security, provider, or access controls.

## Your data

Application email is encrypted and separately indexed with a keyed lookup value for duplicate handling. Public pages use a session identifier for limited, allowlisted events; Aurel does not use browser fingerprinting or build a cross-site identity graph. Attribution is retained only when an application is submitted.

Authenticated customers can request an export or deletion of growth and application data from Settings. Deleting growth data does not change product access, blockchain history, provider records, or records that must be retained for security or legal reasons. See the [privacy notice](/legal/privacy-notice/) for details.

## Service status

The application may be open, paused, or closed. A paused form does not create durable submissions. Submitting the same email again returns the same neutral outcome and does not reveal whether an application already exists.
