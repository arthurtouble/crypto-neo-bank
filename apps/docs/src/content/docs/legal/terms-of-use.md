## 4. What Aura does

Aura is a self-custodial money app.

Your Aura account is a wallet on the Base network. Privy, an independent company, creates it when you sign in. You control the wallet through your Privy sign-in. Privy handles sign-in, the key that signs for your wallet, recovery, and key export.

**We never hold your funds or your keys.** We can't move your assets. We can't freeze, recover, or reverse them.

With Aura you can:

- see supported balances and positions on public blockchains;
- send assets on Base to an address or an Aura tag;
- swap assets, and move them to or from other networks. LI.FI, an independent service, finds the routes. It chooses among third-party bridges and exchanges;
- buy and sell crypto, the euro stablecoin, tokenized stocks, and Tether Gold through those same routes;
- supply assets to Aave, a lending protocol on Base, and withdraw them;
- deposit USDC into reviewed Morpho vaults on Base, and withdraw it;
- create a public Aura tag payment page; and
- manage your settings and data, and chat with support.

We may switch these features off. Some may not be available to you yet.

Aura doesn't offer bank accounts, bank transfers, cards, cashback, rewards, or borrowing at the moment. If you see one of these in the app, that doesn't mean it is available.

Aura is not a bank, broker, custodian, exchange, insurer, fiduciary, or investment adviser, unless a screen expressly says otherwise.

## 5. How actions work

When you ask for an action, Aura prepares the exact transaction. Your wallet shows it to you. You decide whether to sign it. Aura doesn't sign for you.

We may pay the network fee for actions Aura prepares. We can stop at any time. If we do, you may need to pay network fees yourself.

## 6. Checks and controls

Before Aura prepares an action, we check that the feature is switched on. We also apply any controls you have turned on. Your controls are:

- an account lock;
- a daily limit; and
- a mode that only lets you send to saved recipients, with a waiting period for recipients you've just saved.

All of these are off until you turn them on.

We may also refuse to prepare an action, delay it, apply our own limits, or switch a feature off. We may do this for security, legal, or partner reasons.

These checks only cover actions prepared through Aura. They can't stop a transaction you sign somewhere else. That includes a transaction you sign with a key you have exported.
