**Version:** 2026-10-01

Digital assets can lose most or all of their value. Only use assets you can afford to lose. Get independent advice when you need it.

## Self-custody and your account

Your Aura account is a wallet that you control through your Privy sign-in. Aura doesn't hold your funds or your keys. So Aura can't recover access for you, and can't reverse a valid blockchain transaction.

Anyone who gets control of your sign-in or recovery methods may be able to control your wallet. If your device, email account, sign-in method, or recovery path is compromised, that can lead to loss.

Your access depends on Privy. If Privy is unavailable, or changes or ends its service, you may not be able to reach your wallet through Aura for a time. Learn how Privy's recovery and export work before you hold significant value.

## Wallet software and network fees

Your account relies on smart-contract wallet software, and on services that send its transactions to the network. These can contain bugs or stop working.

Aura may pay network fees on Base for actions it prepares. We can stop at any time. If we do, you will need the network's fee asset to make transactions.

## Transactions

Aura uses public blockchains and real assets. A transaction can:

- fail;
- be delayed or reordered;
- be front-run, meaning another transaction is placed ahead of yours; or
- cost more than you expected.

A wrong address, network, token, contract, or approval can cause permanent loss.

## Smart contracts and protocols

Contracts can contain bugs. They can be exploited, paused, upgraded, or changed through governance in ways you didn't expect. Price feeds, blockchain data services, validators, and user interfaces can fail.

Aura doesn't offer borrowing. If you use the same wallet with another app to borrow, the protocol can sell your collateral automatically. Aura can't warn you in time or stop it.

## Stablecoins

A stablecoin can lose its target price, become hard to sell, be frozen, or fail. This can happen because of problems with its issuer, reserves, banks, custodians, governance, regulation, or redemption process.

"Stable" doesn't mean guaranteed.

## Earn

Aave and Morpho vault rates are variable. They can fall to zero. Losses on the token, fees, tax, difficulty withdrawing, or protocol failure can outweigh them.

A position in Aave or a Morpho vault is not a bank deposit. Deposit insurance doesn't cover it, unless a specific regulated product says otherwise.

## Swaps and moves between networks

Swaps and moves between networks use routes found by LI.FI. LI.FI chooses among third-party bridges and exchanges. Neither LI.FI nor Aura controls them.

A route can depend on several contracts, liquidity sources, relayers, and networks. Confirmation on the first network doesn't prove the asset arrived on the second. If something goes wrong, recovery can be slow or impossible.

## Markets, liquidity, and prices

Prices can move fast or be manipulated. A quote can go out of date, and liquidity can disappear. Large trades can move the price. A value shown in Aura may not be a price you can actually sell at.

## Providers and regulation

These can change, suspend, or end their services:

- Privy;
- Base and Ethereum;
- Aave;
- Morpho and the vault curators;
- LI.FI, and the bridges and exchanges it uses;
- stablecoin issuers; and
- future partners.

Laws, sanctions, tax rules, or a provider's rules can restrict access, transfers, redemption, or recovery.

## Tokenized assets

A token may not give you the legal rights its name suggests. Your rights and the token's value can be affected by:

- the issuer's insolvency;
- transfer restrictions;
- market closures;
- corporate actions;
- how the underlying asset is held; and
- which laws apply.

Aura supports ten tokenized stocks issued by Coinbase, and Tether Gold.

A Coinbase tokenized stock is not a share in your name. Coinbase says they are only for eligible people outside the US.

Tether Gold depends on Tether holding the gold behind it.

## Operations

Software, networks, data, providers, and people can fail. Security controls reduce risk. They don't remove it.

Keep your own records. Don't rely on Aura as the only way to reach your wallet or to understand a position.
