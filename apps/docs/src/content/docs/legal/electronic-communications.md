**Version:** 2026-10-02

When you accept the terms of use, you agree to receive agreements, disclosures, notices, confirmations, and other records electronically, where the law allows.

## How we deliver records

We deliver records in the app. We also send notices:

- by email, to the email on your account. Every account has one: if you sign in with Telegram or a wallet, Aura asks you to add one before you continue; and
- as browser notifications, in the browsers where you turn them on.

A partner you use may send its own records under its own terms. Bridge is one example, once it is live.

Keep your sign-in email up to date.

## Messages you control

| Message | Your choice |
| --- | --- |
| Security notices, for example when you lock your account, loosen a control, save a new recipient, or close your account | Always sent by email and in the app, because they protect your account. Also sent as a browser notification where you turned those on |
| Transaction notices by email or browser notification: money you receive, and when something you send, swap, or earn completes or fails | On by default. Change them in Settings → Notifications |
| Product-update emails | Off unless you turn them on in Settings → Notifications |

## What you need

- A current browser.
- Internet access.
- A working email address.
- Software that can show and save web pages and PDF files.

## Paper copies and withdrawing consent

**The final policy will explain how to ask for a paper copy, any fee for it, and how to withdraw your consent to electronic delivery without losing rights.**

Where the law allows, a partner may require electronic delivery for a product it offers only online.

Withdrawing consent doesn't affect records already delivered or transactions already completed.
