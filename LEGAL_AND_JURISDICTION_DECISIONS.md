## Offshore incorporation

An offshore entity is not inherently necessary or sufficient for a lawful product. It may be chosen for governance, investment funds, token structures or tax reasons, but it does not erase rules in the countries where customers are solicited or served. Choose the entity only after mapping actual activities, founders, staff, customers, providers, banking access, tax residence and regulatory perimeter. Operational credibility and provider approval may favor a jurisdiction with real substance and clear supervision over a nominal offshore structure.
