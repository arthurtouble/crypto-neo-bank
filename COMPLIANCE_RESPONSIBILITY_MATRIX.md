# Compliance and operating responsibility matrix

This is the proposed allocation to negotiate with a regulated provider. It is not effective until reflected in signed contracts, procedures and counsel approval. “Provider lead” never means Aurel has no oversight or escalation duty.

| Activity | Proposed lead | Aurel obligation | Evidence required before launch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer KYC/KYB decision | Provider | Accurate data capture, status display, no circumvention | Approved flow, decision codes, review SLA |
| Sanctions/PEP screening | Provider | Block unavailable features; escalate matches | Screening scope, rescreening cadence, escalation path |
| Source of funds/wealth | Provider | Collect only approved fields; route requests and explain status | Trigger matrix, document channel, retention allocation |
| Transaction monitoring | Provider for provider rails; protocols/on-chain allocation TBD | Detect product abuse, preserve Aurel evidence, act on provider instructions | Rule and alert ownership, case exchange, freeze authority |
| Wallet screening | Contract-dependent | Enforce agreed risk decisions in Aurel paths | Vendor, thresholds, appeal/escalation process |
| Fiat safeguarding/account record | Provider/bank | Use exact approved terminology; reconcile display | Account ownership, safeguarding/insurance wording, daily report |
| Stablecoin custody/conversion | Provider where provider controls it; customer for self-controlled wallets | Clearly separate provider and self-custody states | Asset/network matrix, settlement and insolvency treatment |
| Wallet key security | Privy/customer | Secure app/session boundary; never request recovery secrets | Privy configuration review, recovery and export test |
| Card issuing and authorization | Provider/issuer | Correct controls/status, support routing, no false acceptance promise | Program agreement, event map, authorization and reversal tests |
| Fraud controls | Shared | Device/product controls, abuse limits, support escalation | RACI, 24/7 emergency channel, loss allocation |
| Disputes/chargebacks/returns | Provider operational lead | Intake, evidence delivery and status communication | Timelines, reason codes, provisional credit rules |
| Suspicious activity reporting | Licensed/obligated party | Escalate facts; do not promise outcomes or notify improperly | Counsel-approved escalation procedure |
| Customer complaints | Shared, single front door defined in contract | Traceable intake and timely routing | Complaint policy, regulated escalation, reporting cadence |
| Marketing and product claims | Aurel, with provider approval for provider services | No “bank”, insurance, guaranteed yield or global-availability misstatement | Approved copy library and change-control process |
| Terms/privacy/consent | Aurel plus provider documents | Present effective version and retain consent evidence | Final documents, versioning and withdrawal handling |
| Data protection | Each party for its role | Minimize data, secure transfers, honor requests and incidents | DPA, controller/processor map, retention/deletion schedule |
| Information security | Each party | Protect Aurel systems; oversee vendors; notify incidents | Security review, subprocessor list, notification terms |
| Regulatory change/country availability | Provider and Aurel counsel | Fail closed when approval is absent or withdrawn | Country matrix owner and emergency disable procedure |
| Reconciliation | Provider supplies authoritative records; Aurel compares | Investigate every unexplained gap; never overwrite canon with projection | File/API spec, cadence, break ownership, sign-off |

## Lean-team implication

No universal rule says the company must have a large employee count merely to integrate an API. Partners and regulators care about capability, accountability, separation of incompatible duties, continuity and timely response. One founder may use automation and specialist contractors, but the named accountable functions, backup coverage and independent review still have to exist. A provider performing KYC does not absorb Aurel’s marketing, product, security, complaint, vendor-oversight and data obligations.
